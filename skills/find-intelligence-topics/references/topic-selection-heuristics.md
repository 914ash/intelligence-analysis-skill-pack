# Topic Selection Heuristics

## Favor Topics That Are
- decision-relevant for a real consumer, operator, or planner
- time-bounded enough to analyze rather than endlessly survey
- actor-centered, event-centered, or capability-centered
- likely to change behavior, posture, or resource allocation if answered well
- narrow enough to turn into a clear intelligence question within one more step

## Avoid Topics That Are
- broad subject areas with no decision hook
- static background themes that do not create a judgment task
- questions that are really requests for general education or encyclopedia summaries
- topics with no realistic path to observable indicators or evidence

## Strong Topic Shapes
- actor + intention + timeframe
- capability + target + opportunity window
- network + vulnerability + likely exploitation path
- emerging signal + plausible implication + watch indicators

## Ranking Questions
- Why does this matter now?
- Who would use the answer?
- What action or posture could change if the answer were known?
- Can the topic be narrowed into a principal intelligence question quickly?
- Is there a plausible evidence path, even if the evidence is incomplete?
