# Skills Map

## Entry Point
- [Intelligence Analysis Router](../skills/intelligence-analysis-router/SKILL.md): choose the right chain when the task is ambiguous.

## Core Tradecraft
- [Find Intelligence Topics](../skills/find-intelligence-topics/SKILL.md): Turn a broad mission area into a prioritized set of analyzable intelligence topics with rationale and next-step question seeds.
- [Craft Intelligence Question](../skills/craft-intelligence-question/SKILL.md): Convert a loose request into a precise intelligence question and a supporting question set.
- [Research and Collection](../skills/research-and-collection/SKILL.md): Build a disciplined evidence set without collapsing collection and judgment into the same step.
- [Formal vs Informal Problem Solving](../skills/formal-vs-informal-problem-solving/SKILL.md): Match the problem shape and stakes to the right level of analytical structure.
- [Critical Thinking](../skills/critical-thinking/SKILL.md): Challenge the reasoning, not just the answer.
- [Source Evaluation](../skills/source-evaluation/SKILL.md): Score reporting discipline before any substantive judgment leans on it.
- [Evidence Gap Analysis](../skills/evidence-gap-analysis/SKILL.md): Make uncertainty explicit and convert it into collection priorities.
- [Estimative Probability](../skills/estimative-probability/SKILL.md): Make uncertainty legible, calibrated, and reusable across products.
- [Intelligence Analysis](../skills/intelligence-analysis/SKILL.md): Produce a traceable judgment from evaluated evidence rather than from intuition alone.
- [Misinformation and Disinformation Analysis](../skills/misinformation-disinformation-analysis/SKILL.md): Detect distortion risks before they contaminate the analytic picture.
- [Defend Analytic Conclusion](../skills/defend-analytic-conclusion/SKILL.md): Make the reasoning auditable under scrutiny.

## Writing
- [Intelligence Writing](../skills/intelligence-writing/SKILL.md): Translate analysis into prose without weakening the logic or caveats.
- [BLUF Writing](../skills/bluf-writing/SKILL.md): Produce a lead that tells the consumer the answer before the details.
- [Inverted Pyramid Writing](../skills/inverted-pyramid-writing/SKILL.md): Sequence the product so the reader can stop early without missing the core message.
- [What / So What Statement](../skills/what-so-what-statement/SKILL.md): Compress evidence and implication into one disciplined analytic statement.
- [Key Judgments Writing](../skills/key-judgments-writing/SKILL.md): Produce standalone judgments that can survive extraction from the full product.
- [Analytic Title Writing](../skills/analytic-title-writing/SKILL.md): Give the product a title that signals subject, angle, and stakes cleanly.

## Structured Methods
- [Analysis of Competing Hypotheses](../skills/analysis-of-competing-hypotheses/SKILL.md): Use inconsistency analysis to compare hypotheses rather than merely collecting support for a favorite view.
- [Team A / Team B Analysis](../skills/team-a-team-b-analysis/SKILL.md): Force the strongest opposing case into the record before finalizing the judgment.
- [Alternative Analysis](../skills/alternative-analysis/SKILL.md): Keep the analysis from collapsing around the first plausible story.
- [TRACE Technique](../skills/trace-technique/SKILL.md): Impose structure on ambiguous cases where evidence is weak but action may still be required.
- [Psychological Cascades Analysis](../skills/psychological-cascades-analysis/SKILL.md): Model how beliefs and reactions spread, not just the initiating event.
- [Delphi Method](../skills/delphi-method/SKILL.md): Run a repeatable expert-consensus process without losing question discipline or transparency.
- [Node Link Analysis](../skills/node-link-analysis/SKILL.md): Turn fragmented relational evidence into an explicit network picture that can be challenged and updated.
