# Analytic Standards

This pack adapts the spirit of ICD 203 into a portable review checklist.

## Product-Level Expectations
- Be objective and independent of political or organizational pressure.
- Be timely enough to support the decision at hand.
- Use all relevant available sources, not only the easiest or most familiar ones.
- Make uncertainty, assumptions, and alternatives visible.

## Tradecraft Standards To Show In The Artifact
- Describe the quality and credibility of underlying sources, data, and methods.
- Express uncertainty associated with major judgments.
- Distinguish between underlying reporting, assumptions, and analyst judgments.
- Incorporate analysis of alternatives.
- Address relevance and implications for the consumer.
- Use clear, logical argumentation.
- Explain major change or continuity in the assessment when relevant.
- Make accurate, bounded judgments instead of sprawling claims.
- Use visuals, tables, or matrices when they make the reasoning easier to audit.

## Review Checklist
- Can a reader tell what is known, inferred, assumed, and missing?
- Does the product show why the lead view leads over at least one alternative?
- Is the confidence level justified by source quality and corroboration?
- Are implications linked to the underlying judgment rather than asserted separately?
- Would a future reviewer know what evidence would overturn the assessment?
