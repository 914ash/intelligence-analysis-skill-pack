# Analytical Contract

Apply this contract in every intelligence skill. It is the minimum tradecraft standard for autonomous or human-guided execution inside this pack.

## Non-Negotiable Rules
- Separate fact, inference, assumption, and gap. Never collapse them into one sentence.
- Tie every substantive judgment to specific evidence or a clearly labeled assumption.
- Evaluate both source reliability and claim credibility before weighting an item heavily.
- Compare the lead explanation to at least one live alternative.
- State what future evidence would strengthen, weaken, or overturn the current view.
- Use disciplined confidence language instead of vague hedging.

## Required Output Elements
- **Analytic Question:** The exact question being answered.
- **Lead Assessment:** The current best judgment in plain language.
- **Evidence-to-Claim Traceability:** The strongest evidence that supports or weakens each major claim.
- **Alternative Explanation:** At least one serious competing explanation and why it is not currently the lead view.
- **Gaps and Collection Implications:** What is still unknown and what should be collected next.
- **Watch Items:** Observable developments that would change the analysis.
- **Confidence Statement:** A calibrated confidence term with a brief reason.

## Minimum Analytic Sequence
1. Restate the question and scope boundary.
2. Build or review the evidence ledger.
3. Grade source quality and information quality.
4. Choose the lightest analytic technique that fits the uncertainty and stakes.
5. Produce the lead judgment and alternatives.
6. Write the watch items, gaps, and confidence statement.

## Failure Conditions
- Do not present single-source or weakly sourced claims as settled facts.
- Do not use a writing skill to hide unresolved analytic disagreement.
- Do not claim confidence that is stronger than the evidence base.
- Do not mark a task `blocked` merely because context is imperfect; use assumptions and record `missing_inputs` unless the work is unsafe or impossible.
