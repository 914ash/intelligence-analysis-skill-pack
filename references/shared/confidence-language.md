# Confidence Language

Use disciplined estimative language and avoid false precision.

## Suggested Bands
- Almost certainly: roughly 90-99%
- Very likely: roughly 75-90%
- Likely: roughly 60-75%
- Roughly even chance: roughly 45-55%
- Unlikely: roughly 25-40%
- Very unlikely: roughly 10-25%
- Almost certainly not: roughly 1-10%

## Rules
- Pair the confidence term with a short rationale or caveat.
- Do not use numeric bands unless the consumer benefits from them.
- Lower confidence when evidence is thin, stale, contradictory, or heavily inferred.
