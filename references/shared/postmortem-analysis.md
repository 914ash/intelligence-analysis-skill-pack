# Postmortem Analysis

Use this after a consequential assessment has had time to age. This note is informed by The Intelligence Shop's writing on revisiting published analysis and by the broader forecasting literature it points to.

## Why Run It
- To see whether the judgment, assumptions, and forecast horizon held up.
- To identify whether mistakes came from weak sourcing, weak logic, bad assumptions, or overconfident writing.
- To improve future calibration rather than only celebrate or punish outcomes.

## Review Questions
- Did the original key judgments remain accurate as new reporting emerged?
- Which assumptions held, weakened, or failed?
- Were the probability and confidence labels appropriate?
- Did the product identify indicators that later proved useful or misleading?
- What analytic move should become repeatable next time?
