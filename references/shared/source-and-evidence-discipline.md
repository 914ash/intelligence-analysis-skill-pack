# Source and Evidence Discipline

## Evaluate Every Source On
- Reliability: past accuracy, access, and consistency.
- Credibility: whether the specific claim is plausible, internally coherent, and corroborated.
- Bias and motive: what incentives could distort the reporting.
- Timeliness: whether the information is stale for the question at hand.
- Access path: first-hand, second-hand, aggregated, or inferred.

## Weighting Rules
- Do not treat single-source claims as settled facts.
- Treat anonymous or low-access reporting as provisional until corroborated.
- Note when a source is reliable but the current claim is weak, or vice versa.
- Record what evidence would upgrade or downgrade the claim.
