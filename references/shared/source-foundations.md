This pack was refreshed using the sources listed in `Suggested Refs.txt`. The goal was not to copy those sources blindly, but to turn them into reusable references and templates for future agents while keeping a few public-safe Markdown source mirrors where that improves portability.

## Why These Sources Fit
- They reinforce disciplined uncertainty language, alternative analysis, and source grading.
- They add public-facing writing signals without turning the pack into marketing copy.
- They help the skills point to reusable doctrine instead of burying everything inside individual `SKILL.md` files.
