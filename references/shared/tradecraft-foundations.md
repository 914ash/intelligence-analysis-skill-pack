# Tradecraft Foundations

This note distills the core tradecraft expectations behind the pack. It is informed by ICD 203, the structured analytic techniques primer mirrored at `../source-docs/us-government-structured-analytic-techniques-primer-2009.md`, CIS CTI guidance, and public intelligence-writing examples from The Intelligence Shop, The Cipher Brief, and Semafor.

## What Good Intelligence Work Looks Like
- Start from a scoped question, not a broad theme.
- Distinguish reporting, inference, assumption, and gap so the logic can be audited.
- Treat source quality as part of the answer, not as metadata to bolt on later.
- Compare the lead explanation to at least one live alternative before publishing.
- Explain uncertainty in plain language and state what would change the view.
- Write for decision utility: what is happening, why it matters, and what to watch next.

## Bias Risks To Actively Counter
- **Confirmation bias:** Over-weighting evidence that supports the first plausible explanation.
- **Anchoring:** Letting the opening frame or first report define the answer space.
- **Mirror imaging:** Assuming the target values, fears, and choices resemble the analyst's.
- **Availability bias:** Overestimating vivid, recent, or heavily repeated reporting.
- **Groupthink:** Converging early because dissent is weakly surfaced.
- **Coherence bias:** Forcing scattered facts into a neat story before the evidence warrants it.

## Practical Countermeasures
- Run a key assumptions check before a judgment hardens.
- Record disconfirming evidence and missing evidence, not only supporting evidence.
- Use indicators and watch items to keep the judgment testable over time.
- Select the lightest structured technique that still makes the reasoning visible.
- Run a postmortem on consequential forecasts or judgments after enough time has passed.

## Writing Signals Worth Preserving
- Lead with the assessment, not the scene-setting.
- Keep implication language close to the judgment that drives it.
- Favor short, high-signal paragraphs and informative section labels.
- Avoid hype, thesis inflation, and false decisiveness.

## Operational Standards Reflected In This Pack
- Evidence quality matters as much as analytic elegance.
- Alternatives are mandatory on consequential judgments.
- Confidence is about the strength of the evidence and reasoning, not conviction.
- Writing is the delivery layer for analysis, not a substitute for it.
- Review after publication is part of tradecraft, not a separate management exercise.
